WHY 64496 (not 23456): AS 23456 (AS_TRANS) carries operational meaning during 4-byte ASN
transition — old BGP speakers substitute it for un-representable 4-byte ASNs. AS 64496–64511
is reserved exclusively for documentation (RFC 5398) with no operational semantics, making it
cleaner for lab use and blog narrative.
